No target label, timestamp or operating-block metadata is required or accepted as a predictor. No probability or calibrated confidence is fabricated.

PRESERVED MODEL IDENTITY

Random Forest RF E4

Four physical electrical channels. Five nominal operating-state labels. The original fitted preprocessing and trained model are preserved.

  1. 0 Normal
  2. 1 Short circuit
  3. 2 Resistive degradation
  4. 3 Open circuit
  5. 4 Natural building shade

Class 4 is an environmental condition, not hardware failure. A prediction is not a confirmed physical diagnosis. This research demonstration is not a certified safety-monitoring system.
