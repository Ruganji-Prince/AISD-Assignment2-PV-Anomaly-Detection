Research context & limitations

  • The historical TEST partition was previously inspected. Severe class imbalance and normal-versus-shade confusion limit interpretation of aggregate results.
  • The original model-size and complete-path latency targets remain unmet. Visual changes do not improve model performance.
  • Illustrative synthetic measurements are not validated fault simulations. Confidence is not calibrated; predictions are not confirmed physical diagnoses.
  • Azure scales to zero: the first request may require a cold start. Hardware faults and natural building shade are treated separately.
